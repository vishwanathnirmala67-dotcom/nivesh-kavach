# SANGYAN submission kit

## 1) "Explain your Solution" (limit 500 chars)
NiveshKavach is a privacy-first scam shield for first-time Indian investors. Paste or speak a suspicious message/link in Hindi or English; it flags fraud patterns (guaranteed returns, tips groups, OTP/KYC phishing, look-alike SEBI/bank domains), explains why in simple words, reads it aloud, and shows next steps (1930, cybercrime.gov.in, SEBI SCORES). Fully on-device: no OTP/SMS access, no stock tips. Includes a scam quiz.
